## Section 1: What is Prompt Engineering?

**Prompt Engineering** is the skill of writing clear, specific instructions to get better results from AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini.

Think of it like giving directions to a new employee. If you say "do something useful," they'll guess. If you say "write a 3-bullet summary of this report for my CEO," they'll deliver exactly what you need.

**Why it matters:** AI tools are only as good as the instructions they receive. A vague prompt gives a vague answer. A well-crafted prompt gets you a precise, actionable, high-quality response — saving time, reducing back-and-forth, and unlocking the real power of AI.

---

## Section 2: Weak vs. Improved Prompt Example

| | Weak Prompt | Improved Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| **The Prompt** | "Write something about leadership." | "Write a 150-word LinkedIn post for HR leaders on how AI is changing employee onboarding. Use a confident tone. End with a thought-provoking question." |
| **What AI Gets** | No topic depth, no audience, no format, no length | Clear topic, defined audience, specific format, desired tone, and a call-to-action |
| **Output Quality** | Generic paragraph about leadership | Targeted, professional LinkedIn post ready to publish |

**The difference?** The improved prompt answers five invisible questions: *Who is the audience? What format? What tone? How long? What outcome?*

---

## Section 3: Three Practical Benefits of Better Prompts

1. **Save time** — You get usable output on the first try instead of going back and forth five times to fix it.
2. **Get consistent quality** — Structured prompts produce structured results, especially useful when creating reports, emails, or social content regularly.
3. **Unlock advanced AI capabilities** — Most people use only 20% of what AI can do. Better prompts access the other 80% — role-playing, frameworks, analysis, creativity.
